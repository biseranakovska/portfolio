UI Design Figma 2025

Labos.

Overview

A job searching platform concept designed in Figma — clean, functional, and built for clarity.

Labos. is a UI/UX design concept for a job searching platform, designed entirely in Figma. The focus was on creating a clean, distraction-free experience that puts the job listings front and centre — no clutter, no dark patterns, just a well-structured interface that respects the user's time.

Role UI Design & Development
Year 2025
Stack Figma
Duration 2 weeks
Highlights
  • 01

    Figma design

    A complete UI concept designed in Figma, covering the core flows a job seeker would go through.

  • 02

    Clean information architecture

    Job listings, filters, and detail views designed with clarity as the primary goal, nothing in the way of the content.

  • 03

    Design system thinking

    Consistent type, spacing, and components throughout — designed to scale if it were handed off to a developer.
